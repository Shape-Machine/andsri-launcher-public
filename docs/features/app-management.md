# App management

Users organize apps with favorites, names, and hiding—without folders or complex modes.

## Favorites

- Add or remove an app from its long-press menu or Settings.
- Reorder favorites manually with dedicated drag handles.
- Keep large favorite sets scrollable while editing.

## Apps

- Launch with one tap.
- Rename locally without changing the installed application.
- Open Android App info from the long-press menu.
- Remove uninstalled apps from favorites automatically.

## Hidden apps

- Hide only after confirmation.
- Remove hidden apps from the primary launcher view.
- Require biometric or device-credential authentication every time Settings reveals them.
- Restore selected apps from the protected list.

## Boundaries

- No folders, app groups, work profiles, Private Space, deep shortcuts, or uninstall action.
